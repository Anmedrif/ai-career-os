# Career Lifecycle

AI Career OS treats career development as a set of connected loops rather than a single application pipeline. Each stage produces a controlled output and identifies the source that may be updated.

## Lifecycle map

| Phase | Stage | Primary output | Human gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Establish | 1. Build a verified professional profile | Fact register with evidence status | Approve profile facts |
| Establish | 2. Define career goals | Goal and decision record | Approve material goals |
| Establish | 3. Identify transferable skills | Supported skills map | Confirm interpretations |
| Establish | 4. Identify skills gaps | Gap and evidence table | Accept learning priorities |
| Explore | 5. Research industries and roles | Time-stamped research notes | Approve scope if expanded |
| Explore | 6. Discover opportunities | Candidate opportunity records | Decide whether to assess |
| Explore | 7. Assess opportunity fit | Evidence-based fit assessment | Decide pursue / hold / reject |
| Position | 8. Tailor a CV | Versioned draft | Approve exact content |
| Position | 9. Draft application materials | Draft letter or form answers | Approve exact content |
| Prepare | 10. Prepare interview answers | Evidence-linked answer bank | Approve final answers |
| Prepare | 11. Practise interviews | Practice observations | Decide what to revise |
| Prepare | 12. Prepare employer questions | Prioritized question list | Approve sensitive questions |
| Act | 13. Manage applications | Application and event records | Authorize each external action |
| Decide | 14. Evaluate offers | Structured comparison | Human decision only |
| Transition | 15. Plan relocation or onboarding | Risk and action plan | Approve commitments |
| Develop | 16. Build a portfolio | Reviewed project artifacts | Approve public content |
| Develop | 17. Optimize LinkedIn | Section-by-section drafts | Approve each profile change |
| Develop | 18. Plan learning and certifications | Learning plan | Confirm time and cost |
| Learn | 19. Conduct quarterly reviews | Review record and proposals | Approve strategic changes |
| Govern | 20. Maintain consistency | Cross-material discrepancy log | Approve fact corrections |

## Four operating loops

### Establish

Create the factual and strategic base. Facts and decisions remain separate. A recommendation to pursue a field does not become a goal until the user approves it.

### Explore and position

Research is time-stamped and source-backed. Fit assessment uses the verified profile and records gaps without converting role requirements into candidate experience. Tailoring changes emphasis, not facts.

### Act and decide

An approved draft is still not an authorized external action. Submission, messaging, scheduling, offer acceptance, and profile changes each require a separate authorization and a completion record.

### Learn and govern

Outcomes are observed first. Interpretation, hypothesis, lesson, and recommendation remain distinct. Adopted changes are recorded by the source that owns the affected subject.

## State vocabulary

Every workflow records the four independent state dimensions defined in [Source Governance](SOURCE_GOVERNANCE.md):

- **Evidence state:** directly evidenced, user-confirmed, source-supported, inference, contradictory, unresolved, or historical.
- **Decision state:** proposed, pending human decision, approved within stated scope, rejected, withdrawn, or superseded.
- **Document state:** draft, unapproved, approved exact version, current, historical predecessor, review required, or approved decision pending integration.
- **Execution state:** prepared, content approved, externally authorized, attempted, completed, or outcome recorded.

These dimensions answer different questions and must remain separate. In particular, content approval is not external authorization, authorization is not completion, and completion is not an outcome.

Workflows and templates may additionally use **information-type tags** `FACT`, `DECISION`, `ASSUMPTION`, and `RECOMMENDATION` to describe what a field contains. These tags are not states and never establish evidence strength, approval, currentness, or permission.

Use no bare `APPROVED` label. Record the qualified state that actually applies: `APPROVED WITHIN STATED SCOPE`, `APPROVED EXACT VERSION`, or `CONTENT APPROVED`. No earlier state implies a later one.

## Review rhythm

- Review volatile sources, such as job postings, immediately before consequential use.
- Review the professional profile whenever a material factual correction appears.
- Review goals after a material constraint or career-direction change.
- Review active materials when an upstream fact or decision changes.
- Run the [Quarterly Career Review](workflows/QUARTERLY_CAREER_REVIEW_WORKFLOW.md) on a realistic cadence chosen by the user.
