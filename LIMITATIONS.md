# Limitations

AI Career OS is a reference design and portfolio artifact. Its documentation can improve consistency and review discipline, but it cannot guarantee correct facts, fair decisions, privacy, job-market success, or model reliability.

## Current limitations

- No autonomous agent runtime or production service is included.
- Agent files are operating contracts and review prompts, not evidence of deployed software agents.
- The workflows rely on disciplined source maintenance and human judgment.
- The system cannot independently verify employment, education, legal eligibility, certifications, or achievements.
- External job and market information can become stale and must be refreshed before use.
- The evaluation rubric supports structured review but is not a validated benchmark.
- Synthetic case studies demonstrate method, not measured production performance.
- No automatic redaction, secret scanning, link checking, or Markdown validation tool is shipped in this version.
- No direct LinkedIn, job-board, email, calendar, or GitHub automation is implemented.
- Offer, relocation, immigration, tax, contract, and employment questions require qualified advice where appropriate.
- Bias checks can identify visible risks but cannot prove an output is unbiased.
- Human approval can still be mistaken, incomplete, or based on insufficient evidence.
- The reusable professional-profile starter is blank by design; adopters must supply, verify, and protect their own information.

## Deliberate boundaries

The project does not:

- rank people for employment;
- infer protected characteristics;
- scrape private accounts;
- store credentials;
- reuse real applications as examples;
- train or fine-tune a foundation model;
- claim production-scale deployment; or
- authorize an external action from an approved content draft.

## Responsible use

Treat outputs as decision support. Preserve uncertainty, verify important facts in current sources, involve the affected person, and stop when the required authority or evidence is missing.
