## Grounding Rules

- Use only facts in the synthetic professional profile.
- Describe LLM evaluation as project practice.
- Keep the learning program `In Progress`.

- If asked about an unknown detail, answer honestly and bridge to relevant experience.

I am now building practical experience with LLM-output review through structured projects, while continuing an applied workflow-quality program. ## Question 2: Describe a Time You Handled an Ambiguous Case

### STAR Draft

**Situation:** In the fictional process-quality role, a workflow record matched two possible exception categories.

**Task:** Maya needed to record a consistent decision without exceeding the available guidance.

**Action:** She compared the relevant definitions, documented the evidence for each interpretation, selected the best-supported temporary category, and escalated the ambiguity for specialist review.

**Result:** The case was resolved through the designated review path, and the recorded reasoning made the decision easy to audit.

### Boundary

No volume, percentage, time saving, or employer policy is invented.

## Question 3: What Experience Do You Have With LLM Evaluation?

### Draft Answer

> My LLM-evaluation experience is project-based rather than paid employment. I have practiced comparing outputs with instructions and source passages, identifying unsupported claims and contradictions, and recording reviewer reasoning with a structured rubric. I am still developing the technical side, including beginner Python, so I would present myself as ready to learn within a well-defined quality process rather than as an experienced AI engineer.

### Why This Answer Is Preferred

- Directly answers the question.
- Preserves the paid-work boundary.
- Gives concrete evaluation activities.
- Acknowledges the current technical level.

## Question 4: What Would You Do If Two Sources Conflict?

### Draft Answer

> I would not choose the wording that sounds best. I would record the competing statements, check which source has authority for that fact, review currentness and scope, and escalate if the conflict remained material. If the output had to proceed before resolution, I would omit the disputed claim or label it clearly as unresolved.
