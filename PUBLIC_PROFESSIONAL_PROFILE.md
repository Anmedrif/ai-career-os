# Reusable Professional Profile Starter

> **Neutral starter:** This file contains no real person's identity, employment history, languages, education, certifications, location, contact details, or career goals. It is a structure to complete with your own verified information.

## Purpose

Create one controlled professional profile that other AI Career OS workflows can reference without inventing, upgrading, or duplicating facts.

Keep the working profile private by default. Publish only a deliberately reduced version after a separate privacy review and explicit human approval.

## Setup Rules

1. Replace placeholders only with information you are entitled to use.
2. Attach an evidence status to every material claim.
3. Use `Unknown` or `Not provided` when a fact is missing.
4. Do not infer dates, titles, metrics, credentials, legal status, or proficiency levels.
5. Keep private evidence references out of public outputs.
6. Approve the exact public version separately from approving an application or account change.

## Keep Private by Default

- Personal phone numbers, private email addresses, exact home addresses, and identity documents
- Immigration, work-authorization, medical, financial, housing, and family information
- Private applications, recruiter messages, assessments, offers, and salary records
- Employer or client confidential material
- Account credentials, tokens, cookies, private URLs, and local paths
- Unsupported metrics, private evidence files, and unapproved images

## Publication Gate

Before publishing any completed version:

1. Verify every material claim.
2. Remove private evidence references and unnecessary personal data.
3. Review combinations of facts that could create unintended identification risk.
4. Approve the exact public wording.
5. Authorize publication separately.
