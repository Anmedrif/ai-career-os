## State model

`FACT`, `DECISION`, `ASSUMPTION`, and `RECOMMENDATION` are compact information-type tags only. Every material entry must separately record the applicable **Evidence**, **Decision**, **Document**, and **Execution** states from [Source Governance](../SOURCE_GOVERNANCE.md).

## Review controls

- [ ] Requirements are current and traceable.
- [ ] Direct, transferable, learning, and self-reported evidence are separated.
- [ ] No proficiency is upgraded because a course was started.
- [ ] Each priority has an observable validation method.
- [ ] Time, cost, and market expectations remain labeled assumptions.
- [ ] The professional approved priorities and resource commitments.
