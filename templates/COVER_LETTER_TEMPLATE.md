## State model

`FACT`, `DECISION`, `ASSUMPTION`, and `RECOMMENDATION` are compact information-type tags only. Every material entry must separately record the applicable **Evidence**, **Decision**, **Document**, and **Execution** states from [Source Governance](../SOURCE_GOVERNANCE.md). Final claims require adequate evidence and any governing decision must be `Approved within stated scope`.

## Quality review

- [ ] Addresses the specific role and essential requirements.
- [ ] Adds evidence rather than repeating the CV.
- [ ] Contains no invented organization knowledge or enthusiasm claims.
- [ ] Uses only verified dates, titles, skills, outcomes, and metrics.
- [ ] Distinguishes transferable from direct experience.
- [ ] Contains no unnecessary personal or third-party data.
- [ ] Meets requested language, length, and format.
- [ ] Matches the approved CV and profile.
