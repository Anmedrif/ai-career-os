# LinkedIn About Workflow

## Objective

Develop a clear first-person About section that connects verified experience, transferable strengths, current development, and career direction.

## Recommended structure

1. **Opening:** present professional identity and direction in two concise sentences.
2. **Evidence:** summarize two or three supported experience themes.
3. **Strengths:** connect relevant skills to the target role.
4. **Development:** state current learning accurately, including `In Progress` where required.
5. **Direction:** describe the type of work or problems of interest.
6. **Close:** add a neutral invitation to connect only if approved.

## Process

1. Review the existing About text.
2. Map every factual clause to the verified profile.
3. Remove repetition, unsupported adjectives, and confidential detail.
4. Draft one version in professional English.
5. Read the opening separately because it may be visible before “see more.”
6. Check consistency with the headline, Experience, CV, GitHub, and interview positioning.
7. Score the draft with the [LLM rubric](../evaluation/LLM_OUTPUT_EVALUATION_RUBRIC.md).
8. Request approval of the exact text.

## Quality questions

- Is the transition described without erasing the person's established experience?
- Does each capability have a source?
- Are self-assessed skills labeled proportionately?
- Is the tone confident without exaggeration?
- Does the section avoid sensitive contact, legal, salary, or relocation details?
- Is the project contribution accurate about AI-assisted development?

## Stop conditions

Stop if target positioning is not approved, employment facts conflict, the text requires an unsupported achievement, or the user has not confirmed whether an item may be public.
