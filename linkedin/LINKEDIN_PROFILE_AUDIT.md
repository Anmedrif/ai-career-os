## Cross-profile checks

- [ ] Headline and About support the same positioning.
- [ ] Experience dates and titles match the verified profile.
- [ ] Skills are supported and relevant.
- [ ] In-progress certifications are not shown as completed.
- [ ] Projects distinguish design, AI assistance, and implemented work.
- [ ] No confidential employer, client, case, tool, or policy detail appears.
- [ ] Contact visibility is intentional.
- [ ] Keywords are natural and supported rather than repeated for search ranking.
- [ ] GitHub, CV, interview answers, and LinkedIn use consistent facts.
- [ ] Every proposed edit has an explicit approval status.

## Discoverability review

Assess:

- target-role terminology in the headline and About opening;
- role-relevant skills in Experience and Skills;
- location and work-model settings, if the user chooses to publish them;
- complete and accurate education and certification fields;
- useful Featured items with clear descriptions;
- accessible media and link previews; and
- whether Open-to-Work visibility matches the user's privacy preference.

## Output

Create one issue list, prioritized as:

1. factual or privacy correction;
2. positioning inconsistency;
3. recruiter-discoverability improvement;
4. clarity or formatting improvement.

Then follow the [LinkedIn Optimization Workflow](../workflows/LINKEDIN_OPTIMIZATION_WORKFLOW.md) one section at a time.
