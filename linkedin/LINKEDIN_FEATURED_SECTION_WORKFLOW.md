# LinkedIn Featured Section Workflow

## Objective

Select a small set of approved public artifacts that demonstrate relevant work and are understandable without private context.

## Candidate criteria

An item should:

- support the target positioning;
- be publicly accessible and owned or lawfully shareable;
- contain no personal or employer-confidential data;
- have an accurate title and description;
- explain the author's contribution and AI assistance;
- render well on mobile and desktop; and
- remain useful if viewed without the rest of the profile.

## Process

1. Inventory candidate links, posts, documents, and projects privately.
2. verify ownership, publication rights, current availability, and privacy.
3. rank candidates by relevance, evidence strength, and clarity.
4. select two to four items.
5. draft a title and one- or two-sentence description for each.
6. test link previews and accessibility.
7. request content approval.
8. request separate authorization before adding items to LinkedIn.

## Suggested project entry

- **Title:** concise project name
- **Type:** repository, article, presentation, or approved media
- **Description:** problem, method, contribution, and limitation
- **Link:** public URL only
- **Evidence:** approval and rights record

Do not feature unapproved placeholders, private repositories, raw AI conversations, real application materials, or images with uncertain rights.
