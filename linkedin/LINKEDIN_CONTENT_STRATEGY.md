# LinkedIn Content Strategy

## Purpose

Use content to demonstrate consistent professional thinking and learning without overstating expertise or disclosing private work.

## Content pillars

1. **Applied learning:** concise lessons from approved courses or experiments.
2. **Quality practice:** examples of instruction following, factual grounding, and error analysis using synthetic data.
3. **Career-system design:** source governance, Human-in-the-Loop controls, and workflow design.
4. **Transferable experience:** general lessons about cross-functional operations, documentation, quality, and escalation after public-use approval.
5. **Portfolio progress:** approved project milestones with honest limitations.

## Cadence

Choose a sustainable cadence rather than a high-volume target. A useful cycle is:

1. draft one evidence-backed idea;
2. review claims and privacy;
3. approve the exact post;
4. publish through an authorized action;
5. observe outcomes without over-attributing cause; and
6. record one evidence-based lesson.

## Post structure

- clear first line;
- one problem or observation;
- two to four useful points;
- an example or artifact when safe;
- a limitation or open question when relevant; and
- a natural close.

## Guardrails

- Do not publish private job-search events.
- Do not copy employer materials or course assessments.
- Do not present AI-generated content as independently authored expertise.
- Do not use engagement as proof of professional competence.
- Do not manufacture certainty, urgency, or personal success.
- Do not publish without exact-content approval.
