# LinkedIn Experience Workflow

## Objective

Create accurate Experience entries that communicate relevant responsibilities and transferable value without exposing confidential work.

## Review unit

Work on one position at a time. Record:

- organization display name and public-use approval;
- functional and contractual title status;
- location and dates;
- supported responsibilities;
- supported outcomes or metrics;
- confidentiality limits; and
- relationship to the target positioning.

## Process

1. Compare the current entry with the verified professional profile.
2. Resolve material date, title, or employer-name conflicts before drafting.
3. Select three to five responsibilities relevant to the positioning.
4. Describe actions and context; add an outcome only when supported.
5. Remove internal tools, policies, case details, client data, and private metrics.
6. Check that a functional title is not presented as a verified legal title when that distinction matters.
7. Review the exact entry for evidence, tone, privacy, and consistency.
8. Request approval before treating the entry as final.

## Writing pattern

- One sentence for role context
- Three to five concise bullets using supported action language
- Optional learning or project-training note, clearly labeled

## Prohibited shortcuts

- Reconstructing responsibilities from a generic job description
- Converting team results into personal achievements
- Adding tools because they are common in the industry
- Publishing approximate metrics as audited results
- Naming a client or project without public-use approval

Editing LinkedIn remains a separate action after content approval.
